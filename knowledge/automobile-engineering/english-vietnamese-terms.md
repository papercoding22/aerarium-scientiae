## How To Study

Study five terms at a time:

1. Read the English term aloud.
2. Read its simple English meaning.
3. Explain it again in Vietnamese without looking.
4. Find the part or function on a real car, photo, or diagram.
5. Make one short English sentence with the term.
6. Review it the next day and one week later.

Vietnamese automobile terms can vary between textbooks, manufacturers, and repair shops. Common alternatives are included when useful.

## Cars And Their Structure

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Simple meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| automobile | ô tô | A road vehicle that usually has four wheels and carries people or goods. | An automobile contains many connected systems. |
| car | xe ô tô; xe con | The everyday English word for an automobile, especially a passenger vehicle. | This car has five seats. |
| vehicle | phương tiện; xe | A general word that includes cars, buses, trucks, motorcycles, and more. | A truck is a vehicle, but it is not usually called a car. |
| component | bộ phận; linh kiện | One part of a larger system. | A sensor is an electronic component. |
| system | hệ thống | A group of parts that work together. | The braking system slows the car. |
| chassis | khung gầm | The main supporting structure and running parts of a vehicle. Its exact meaning changes with context. | The suspension is connected to the chassis. |
| body | thân xe; vỏ xe | The structure around the passengers and cargo. | The body helps protect the people inside. |

## Power And Drivetrain

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Simple meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| engine | động cơ | A machine that converts energy into mechanical motion. In car discussions, it often means a combustion engine. | The engine burns fuel to produce power. |
| internal-combustion engine | động cơ đốt trong | An engine that burns fuel inside its cylinders. | A petrol engine is an internal-combustion engine. |
| electric motor | động cơ điện; mô-tơ điện | A machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical motion. | An electric motor can produce torque from zero speed. |
| powertrain | hệ thống động lực | The group that creates power and delivers it toward the wheels. It usually includes the engine or motor and the drivetrain. | An electric-car powertrain can include a battery, inverter, motor, and reduction gear. |
| drivetrain | hệ thống truyền động; hệ thống truyền lực | The parts that carry power from the engine or motor to the driven wheels. | The drivetrain includes shafts and a differential. |
| transmission | hộp số; bộ truyền động | A device that changes the relationship between input speed, torque, and output speed. | The transmission selects a suitable gear ratio. |
| clutch | ly hợp; côn | A device that connects or disconnects power between rotating parts. | The driver presses the clutch pedal before changing gear. |
| differential | bộ vi sai | A gear mechanism that lets driven wheels rotate at different speeds while turning. | The differential helps the car turn smoothly. |
| axle | trục; cầu xe, depending on context | A shaft or structural assembly connected to the wheels. | Torque can travel through an axle shaft to a wheel. |

## Important Differences

### Engine And Motor

In everyday English, these words sometimes overlap. In modern car discussions:

- `engine` usually means a combustion engine
- `motor` usually means an electric motor

Both are commonly translated as `động cơ` in Vietnamese, so the context matters.

### Power And Torque

- `torque` is turning force
- `power` describes how quickly work is done

Think of torque as how strongly you turn a wrench. Power also considers how quickly you can keep turning it.

### Powertrain And Drivetrain

- `powertrain` includes the parts that create power and deliver it
- `drivetrain` focuses on the parts that deliver power to the driven wheels

The exact boundary can vary between companies and technical documents. Check how each source defines the terms.

### Wheel And Tire

- `wheel` is the hard rotating structure, including the rim
- `tire` or British `tyre` is the rubber part around the wheel

In casual speech, `wheel` can mean both parts together.

### Brake And Break

- `brake` means the system or action used to slow a vehicle: `phanh`
- `break` means to damage, separate, or stop working: `làm vỡ`, `làm hỏng`, or `bị hỏng`

They sound the same but have different spellings and meanings.

## Useful Sentence Patterns

- `The ___ converts ___ into ___.`
  - The motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
- `The ___ transfers power from ___ to ___.`
  - The drivetrain transfers power from the motor to the wheels.
- `The ___ controls ___.`
  - The steering system controls the car's direction.
- `The ___ measures ___.`
  - The sensor measures wheel speed.
- `The purpose of the ___ is to ___.`
  - The purpose of the differential is to allow different wheel speeds.
- `A possible cause of this fault is ___.`
  - A possible cause of this fault is a damaged wire.
