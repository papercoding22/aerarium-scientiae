# Chassis, Steering, Suspension, And Vehicle-Dynamics Terms

These terms describe how a vehicle supports loads, responds to road inputs, turns, and remains stable.

## Chassis And Suspension Parts

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| chassis | khung gầm | Structural and running-system foundation of a vehicle. Exact boundary varies by company and context. | May include frame/body structure, suspension, steering, brakes, and wheels. |
| suspension | hệ thống treo | System connecting wheels to the sprung structure while controlling relative motion and loads. | Must support, guide, isolate, and maintain tire contact. |
| independent suspension | hệ thống treo độc lập | Suspension in which one wheel can move vertically with limited direct geometric effect on the opposite wheel. | Front and rear designs differ. |
| dependent suspension | hệ thống treo phụ thuộc | Suspension in which left and right wheel motions are mechanically linked by an axle or beam. | Includes live-axle and some beam designs. |
| semi-independent suspension | hệ thống treo bán độc lập | Suspension with controlled coupling between left and right wheels. | Torsion-beam rear suspension is a common example. |
| MacPherson strut | hệ thống treo kiểu MacPherson | Independent suspension using a load-carrying strut as an upper wheel-location member. | Compact and common at the front. |
| double wishbone | hệ thống treo tay đòn kép | Independent suspension locating the upright with upper and lower control arms. | Also called double A-arm. |
| multi-link suspension | hệ thống treo đa liên kết | Independent suspension using several links to control wheel motion and compliance. | Link count and function vary. |
| torsion-beam suspension | hệ thống treo dầm xoắn | Rear suspension with trailing arms connected by a beam that twists in roll. | Semi-independent behaviour. |
| solid axle | cầu cứng | Rigid member connecting left and right wheel positions. | Can be driven or non-driven. |
| control arm | tay đòn điều khiển; càng treo | Link locating a wheel carrier relative to body or subframe. | Also called wishbone or suspension arm, depending on geometry. |
| trailing arm | tay đòn dọc | Suspension arm extending mainly longitudinally from its body pivot to wheel carrier. | Controls fore-aft wheel position. |
| lateral link | thanh liên kết ngang | Suspension link mainly controlling lateral wheel location. | Common in multi-link systems. |
| knuckle | trụ đứng; ngõng trục | Wheel-carrying member connecting hub, suspension links, and often steering tie rod. | Also called upright or steering knuckle. |
| wheel hub | moay-ơ bánh xe | Rotating component supporting and mounting the wheel. | Runs on wheel bearings. |
| ball joint | khớp cầu | Spherical joint allowing angular motion while transmitting suspension or steering load. | Wear creates play and alignment change. |
| bushing | bạc cao su; ống lót đàn hồi | Compliant joint isolating noise and allowing controlled movement between parts. | Rubber, elastomer, hydraulic, or solid types. |
| subframe | khung phụ | Detachable structural assembly carrying suspension, steering, or powertrain loads. | Isolated or rigidly attached to body. |
| anti-roll bar | thanh cân bằng; thanh chống lắc | Torsion spring coupling left and right suspension motion to resist body roll. | Also called stabilizer bar or sway bar. |
| strut brace | thanh giằng tháp giảm xóc | Structural brace linking suspension mounting regions to reduce body deflection. | Effect depends on body stiffness and load path. |

## Suspension Geometry

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wheel alignment | góc đặt bánh xe | Set of geometric wheel and steering-axis settings. | Includes camber, caster, and toe; vehicle conditions must be specified. |
| camber angle | góc camber; góc nghiêng bánh xe | Angle between wheel plane and vertical when viewed from front or rear. | Degrees (`°`); sign convention must be stated. |
| caster angle | góc caster; góc nghiêng dọc trục lái | Angle of steering axis from vertical in side view. | Degrees (`°`); positive caster generally adds self-aligning tendency. |
| toe angle | góc chụm | Difference in wheel heading relative to vehicle centerline or each other. | Toe-in versus toe-out; degrees or millimetres under a stated method. |
| steering-axis inclination (SAI) | góc nghiêng ngang trục lái | Inward inclination of steering axis from vertical in front view. | `SAI`; also called kingpin inclination. |
| scrub radius | bán kính chà | Ground-plane distance between steering-axis intersection and tire contact-center reference. | Millimetre (`mm`); sign convention matters. |
| mechanical trail | độ kéo sau cơ học | Ground-plane longitudinal distance between steering-axis intersection and tire contact reference. | Contributes to steering torque and self-alignment. |
| wheel offset | độ lệch mâm | Distance between wheel mounting face and wheel center plane. | `ET` is common marking; millimetre (`mm`). |
| roll center | tâm lắc ngang | Kinematic reference point used to analyse lateral-force transfer between body and suspension. | Front and rear roll centers define a roll axis. |
| roll axis | trục lắc ngang | Line connecting front and rear suspension roll centers. | Used in simplified roll analysis. |
| instant center | tâm quay tức thời | Point about which a body or suspension member is instantaneously rotating in a chosen view. | Constructed from suspension-link geometry. |
| camber gain | mức thay đổi camber | Change in camber as suspension moves or body rolls. | Degrees per millimetre or degrees per degree. |
| toe change | thay đổi độ chụm | Change in toe with suspension travel, steering, or compliance. | Degrees or millimetres. |
| bump steer | tự đổi hướng khi hệ treo nhún | Unintended toe change caused by suspension vertical travel. | Described by toe versus wheel travel. |
| compliance steer | tự đổi hướng do biến dạng đàn hồi | Wheel steer angle caused by elastic deformation under force. | Depends on bushings, links, structure, and tire forces. |
| anti-dive geometry | hình học chống chúi | Suspension geometry using force paths to reduce braking-induced body pitch motion. | Expressed as percent under a defined calculation. |
| anti-squat geometry | hình học chống chồm | Suspension geometry using drive-force paths to reduce acceleration-induced rear compression. | Percent under a defined calculation. |
| suspension travel | hành trình hệ thống treo | Available relative wheel movement between limits. | Millimetre (`mm`). Includes jounce and rebound. |
| jounce | hành trình nén; nhún | Suspension movement in which wheel and body move closer. | Also called bump or compression. |
| rebound | hành trình trả; giãn | Suspension movement in which wheel and body move farther apart after compression. | Also called droop in some contexts. |

## Springs Dampers And Ride

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| spring | lò xo; phần tử đàn hồi | Component storing mechanical energy and supporting load through elastic deformation. | Force-displacement relation defines spring rate. |
| coil spring | lò xo trụ | Helical metal spring compressed or extended along its axis. | Common in passenger-car suspension. |
| leaf spring | nhíp lá | Stack or single strip spring that can support load and locate an axle. | Common in commercial vehicles and some rear suspensions. |
| torsion bar | thanh xoắn | Straight spring working mainly in torsion. | One end is anchored; the other follows suspension motion. |
| air spring | lò xo khí; bầu hơi | Flexible chamber using compressed gas as the spring medium. | Enables load levelling and variable ride height. |
| spring rate | độ cứng lò xo | Change in spring force divided by change in spring deflection. | `k`; `N/mm` or `N/m`. May be progressive. |
| wheel rate | độ cứng quy đổi tại bánh xe | Effective suspension stiffness measured at the wheel. | Depends on spring rate, motion ratio, and geometry. |
| motion ratio | tỷ số chuyển động hệ treo | Relationship between wheel travel and spring or damper travel. | Definition convention varies; state it before calculation. |
| damper | bộ giảm chấn | Device dissipating motion energy to control suspension and body oscillation. | Commonly called shock absorber. |
| shock absorber | giảm xóc | Common name for a suspension damper. It controls motion; it does not mainly support static load. | Hydraulic or magnetorheological types are common. |
| compression damping | giảm chấn khi nén | Damper force during decreasing damper length. | Also called bump damping. |
| rebound damping | giảm chấn khi trả | Damper force during increasing damper length. | Often tuned differently from compression. |
| damping ratio | tỷ số giảm chấn | Actual damping relative to critical damping in a simplified system. | `ζ`; dimensionless. |
| critical damping | giảm chấn tới hạn | Minimum damping that returns a simple system without oscillation. | Simplified theoretical reference. |
| ride frequency | tần số dao động thân xe | Natural frequency of a sprung-mass ride mode under a simplified model. | Hertz (`Hz`). Front and rear values may differ. |
| ride comfort | độ êm dịu chuyển động | Human perception of vibration, impact, motion, noise, and seating response. | Evaluated objectively and subjectively. |
| road holding | khả năng bám đường | Ability to maintain useful tire contact and force generation over road disturbances. | Not identical to ride comfort. |
| bottoming | chạm giới hạn nén | Suspension reaching or strongly engaging its compression limit. | Often controlled by a bump stop. |
| bump stop | cao su chặn hành trình | Progressive element limiting suspension compression and adding stiffness near end travel. | Can be an active part of spring-rate tuning. |

## Steering

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| steering system | hệ thống lái | System converting driver or controller command into road-wheel angle. | Includes steering input, gear, linkage, assist, and control. |
| steering wheel | vô lăng | Driver hand control used to request steering angle or torque. | Angle in degrees; torque in `N·m`. |
| steering column | trục lái; cột lái | Shaft and support assembly connecting steering wheel to steering gear or sensor. | Includes collapse and safety features. |
| steering rack | thanh răng lái | Translating rack in a rack-and-pinion steering gear. | Moves tie rods left and right. |
| pinion | bánh răng nhỏ; bánh răng lái | Small gear engaging the steering rack. | Converts rotary input to linear rack travel. |
| tie rod | thanh kéo lái | Link transmitting rack or steering-gear movement to the knuckle. | Inner and outer joints allow articulation. |
| steering arm | đòn lái | Lever on a knuckle or steering mechanism that converts linkage movement into wheel steer. | Geometry affects steering ratio and Ackermann behaviour. |
| steering ratio | tỷ số truyền lái | Ratio between steering-wheel angle and road-wheel steer angle under a stated definition. | Dimensionless, often written `x:1`. May vary with position. |
| rack travel | hành trình thanh răng | Linear displacement of the steering rack. | Millimetre (`mm`). |
| electric power steering (EPS) | trợ lực lái điện | Steering assist generated by an electric motor under electronic control. | `EPS`; column, pinion, or rack assist. |
| hydraulic power steering (HPS) | trợ lực lái thủy lực | Steering assist generated by pressurized hydraulic fluid. | `HPS`; pump may be engine or electrically driven. |
| steer-by-wire | lái bằng tín hiệu điện | Steering architecture where command is transmitted electronically without a continuous mechanical path in normal control. | Requires redundancy and safe-state strategy. |
| Ackermann steering geometry | hình học lái Ackermann | Geometry making inner and outer steered wheels use different angles in a turn. | Ideal relationship depends on wheelbase and track. |
| steering feel | cảm giác lái | Driver perception of steering torque, response, friction, damping, and road information. | Subjective and objective measures are combined. |
| self-aligning torque | mô-men tự lựa của lốp | Tire-generated moment tending to align the steered wheel under many conditions. | `M_z`; `N·m`. Changes with slip angle and load. |
| turning circle | đường kính vòng quay | Diameter of the smallest circular path defined by a stated vehicle point at full steering lock. | `m`; curb-to-curb and wall-to-wall differ. |

## Vehicle Dynamics

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| longitudinal dynamics | động lực học dọc | Study of acceleration, braking, grade, and forces along the vehicle's forward axis. | Vehicle x-axis convention should be stated. |
| lateral dynamics | động lực học ngang | Study of cornering, sideslip, yaw, and lateral forces. | Vehicle y-axis convention should be stated. |
| vertical dynamics | động lực học thẳng đứng | Study of ride, heave, wheel hop, and road-input response. | Vehicle z-axis convention should be stated. |
| roll | lắc ngang; quay quanh trục dọc | Rotation about the vehicle longitudinal axis. | Roll angle `φ`; degrees or radians. |
| pitch | chúc/ngóc; quay quanh trục ngang | Rotation about the vehicle lateral axis. | Pitch angle `θ`. |
| yaw | quay hướng; quay quanh trục đứng | Rotation about the vehicle vertical axis. | Yaw angle `ψ`; yaw rate `r` in `rad/s` or `°/s`. |
| heave | dao động tịnh tiến thẳng đứng | Vertical translation of the sprung body. | Displacement in `m` or `mm`. |
| sideslip angle | góc trượt ngang của xe | Angle between vehicle heading and velocity direction at a defined point. | `β`; degrees or radians. |
| tire slip angle | góc trượt bên của lốp | Angle between wheel heading and tire contact-patch velocity direction. | `α`; sign convention matters. |
| slip ratio | tỷ lệ trượt dọc | Normalized difference between wheel circumferential speed and vehicle longitudinal speed. | `κ` or `s`; definition and sign vary. |
| lateral acceleration | gia tốc ngang | Vehicle acceleration perpendicular to forward direction. | `a_y`; `m/s²` or multiples of `g`. |
| yaw rate | tốc độ quay vòng quanh trục đứng | Rate of change of yaw angle. | `r`; `rad/s` or `°/s`. |
| cornering stiffness | độ cứng góc trượt | Slope of lateral tire force versus slip angle in the near-linear region. | `C_α`; `N/rad`. Sign convention varies. |
| understeer | thiếu lái | Handling condition where more steering input is needed as lateral acceleration rises, under a formal steady-state definition. | Commonly feels as if the car turns less than requested. |
| oversteer | thừa lái | Handling condition where less steering input is needed as lateral acceleration rises, under a formal definition. | Can progress toward rear-axle loss of stability. |
| neutral steer | lái trung tính | Idealized condition with zero understeer gradient. | A formal steady-state concept. |
| understeer gradient | gradient thiếu lái | Change in required steer angle beyond kinematic steer per unit lateral acceleration. | Often `deg/g` or `rad/(m/s²)`. |
| load transfer | chuyển tải trọng động | Redistribution of normal loads among tires due to acceleration and force height. | Total lateral transfer is governed by mass, acceleration, track, and CG height. |
| weight transfer | chuyển tải trọng | Common term for load transfer, although vehicle mass itself does not move between tires. | Prefer `load transfer` in precise engineering writing. |
| longitudinal load transfer | chuyển tải dọc | Change in front and rear axle normal loads during acceleration or braking. | Depends on mass, longitudinal acceleration, CG height, and wheelbase. |
| lateral load transfer | chuyển tải ngang | Change in left and right tire normal loads during cornering. | Depends on lateral acceleration, track, CG, and roll system. |
| traction circle | vòng tròn bám | Simplified diagram of combined longitudinal and lateral tire-force capability. | Actual boundary is often not a perfect circle. |
| friction ellipse | elip ma sát | Model showing trade-off between longitudinal and lateral tire forces. | Shape depends on tire and condition. |
| handling | tính năng điều khiển và ổn định | Vehicle response to steering, acceleration, braking, wind, and road inputs. | Includes objective response and subjective driver assessment. |
| stability | tính ổn định | Tendency to maintain or recover controlled motion after disturbance. | Meaning depends on longitudinal, lateral, roll, or control context. |
| transient response | đáp ứng quá độ | Time-dependent response while a vehicle changes from one state to another. | Examples: turn-in, lane change, throttle lift. |
| steady-state response | đáp ứng xác lập | Behaviour after major transient effects have settled under constant input or path. | Used in constant-radius and constant-speed analysis. |
