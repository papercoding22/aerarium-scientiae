# Brakes, Wheels, And Tires Terms

## Brake Components

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| service brake | phanh chính; phanh chân | Main braking system used to control speed during normal driving. | Usually acts on all wheels. |
| parking brake | phanh đỗ; phanh tay | System holding a stationary vehicle. | Mechanical or electric actuation. |
| emergency brake | phanh khẩn cấp | Braking function or system intended for failure or emergency conditions. | Do not assume it is identical to the parking brake. |
| brake pedal | bàn đạp phanh | Driver input controlling requested braking. | Measured by travel, force, or requested deceleration. |
| brake booster | bộ trợ lực phanh | Device increasing force applied to the master cylinder. | Vacuum, hydraulic, or electric. |
| master cylinder | xi lanh chính phanh; tổng phanh | Hydraulic device converting pedal force and travel into brake-fluid pressure and displacement. | Usually uses separated circuits for redundancy. |
| brake fluid | dầu phanh | Hydraulic fluid transferring pressure and lubricating brake components. | Hygroscopic glycol-based and silicone types differ; use specified grade. |
| brake line | ống dẫn dầu phanh | Rigid or flexible line carrying pressurized brake fluid. | Flexible hose permits wheel movement. |
| caliper | cùm phanh; heo dầu | Disc-brake assembly applying pads to a rotor. | Fixed or floating/sliding caliper. |
| brake piston | piston phanh | Hydraulic piston converting fluid pressure into pad or shoe force. | Seal design also retracts slightly after release. |
| brake pad | má phanh đĩa | Friction material and backing plate pressed against a brake disc. | Compound affects friction, wear, noise, and temperature range. |
| brake disc | đĩa phanh | Rotating disc clamped by brake pads to create friction torque. | Also called brake rotor. |
| ventilated disc | đĩa phanh thông gió | Brake disc with internal air passages improving heat rejection. | Different from drilled or slotted surface features. |
| brake drum | trống phanh; tang trống | Rotating cylindrical friction surface used with internal brake shoes. | Expands thermally during braking. |
| brake shoe | guốc phanh | Curved friction component pressed against a brake drum. | Leading and trailing shoe behaviour can differ. |
| wheel cylinder | xi lanh bánh xe | Hydraulic actuator pushing drum-brake shoes outward. | Contains pistons and seals. |
| friction material | vật liệu ma sát | Engineered material producing controlled friction at the brake interface. | Performance depends on temperature, pressure, speed, and condition. |
| brake rotor runout | độ đảo đĩa phanh | Axial variation of disc position during rotation. | Millimetre (`mm`); can contribute to pedal pulsation. |
| disc thickness variation (DTV) | độ biến thiên chiều dày đĩa | Difference in disc thickness around the rotor. | `DTV`; micrometre or millimetre. |

## Brake Control And Performance

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| braking torque | mô-men phanh | Torque opposing wheel rotation. | `T_b`; `N·m`. |
| braking force | lực phanh | Longitudinal tire-road force slowing the vehicle. | `F_b`; newton (`N`). |
| deceleration | gia tốc chậm dần | Acceleration opposite the direction of travel, reducing speed. | `m/s²` or multiples of `g`. |
| stopping distance | quãng đường dừng | Distance from a defined brake-command point until the vehicle stops. | Includes or excludes driver reaction distance only if stated. Unit `m`. |
| brake balance | phân bố lực phanh | Distribution of braking demand or force between axles or wheels. | Changes with load transfer and control action. |
| brake bias | thiên lệch phanh; tỷ lệ phân bố phanh | Designed or controlled front-to-rear braking proportion. | Static hardware and dynamic electronic control can both contribute. |
| anti-lock braking system (ABS) | hệ thống chống bó cứng phanh | Control system modulating brake pressure to manage excessive wheel slip during braking. | `ABS`; helps preserve steerability and stability. |
| hydraulic control unit (HCU) | cụm điều khiển thủy lực | ABS/ESC assembly containing valves, pump, and hydraulic passages. | `HCU`; controlled by an ECU. |
| electronic brake-force distribution (EBD) | phân phối lực phanh điện tử | Function controlling brake-force distribution, often using ABS hardware. | `EBD`. |
| electronic stability control (ESC) | cân bằng điện tử; điều khiển ổn định điện tử | System using individual wheel braking and sometimes powertrain control to influence vehicle yaw and stability. | `ESC`; naming varies by manufacturer. |
| traction control system (TCS) | hệ thống kiểm soát lực kéo | System limiting excessive driven-wheel slip during acceleration. | `TCS`; can reduce torque or apply brakes. |
| brake assist | hỗ trợ phanh khẩn cấp | Function increasing braking during detected emergency pedal use. | Also called emergency brake assist. |
| brake-by-wire | phanh điều khiển bằng tín hiệu điện | System where electrical signals transmit or coordinate brake demand. | Architectures may retain hydraulic backup. |
| regenerative braking | phanh tái sinh | Electric-machine braking that recovers part of vehicle kinetic energy. | Available torque depends on system limits. |
| blended braking | phanh phối hợp | Control combining regenerative and friction braking. | Must manage total deceleration and pedal feel. |
| brake fade | suy giảm hiệu quả phanh | Reduction in braking effectiveness after high thermal load or repeated braking. | Can involve pad friction, fluid boiling, or drum expansion. |
| brake judder | rung giật khi phanh | Oscillatory force, pedal, steering, or body response during braking. | Can relate to rotor variation, friction, mounting, or suspension. |
| pedal feel | cảm giác bàn đạp | Driver perception of pedal force, travel, stiffness, response, and modulation. | Evaluated subjectively and with force-travel curves. |
| coefficient of friction | hệ số ma sát | Ratio relating tangential friction force to normal load in a simplified model. | `μ`; dimensionless and condition-dependent. |
| brake specific energy | năng lượng phanh riêng | Braking energy normalized by a chosen mass, area, or component basis. | Definition and unit must be stated. |

## Wheel Terms

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wheel | bánh xe; mâm xe | Rotating structural assembly on which a tire is mounted. | In casual speech, may include the tire. |
| rim | vành; mâm | Outer wheel portion that supports tire beads. | Diameter and width commonly stated in inches. |
| wheel center | phần tâm mâm | Central structural portion joining rim to hub mounting face. | Cast, forged, stamped, or fabricated. |
| wheel diameter | đường kính mâm | Nominal tire-seat diameter of a wheel. | Usually inch (`in`), not overall tire diameter. |
| wheel width | chiều rộng mâm | Nominal distance between rim bead-seat flanges under a standard definition. | Usually inch (`in`). |
| wheel offset | độ lệch mâm | Distance between wheel center plane and hub mounting face. | Millimetre (`mm`); positive and negative sign conventions are standardised by context. |
| pitch-circle diameter (PCD) | đường kính vòng bu-lông | Diameter of the circle through wheel fastener centers. | `PCD`; example `5×114.3` means five holes on 114.3 mm circle. |
| center bore | lỗ tâm mâm | Central wheel opening fitting around the vehicle hub pilot. | Millimetre (`mm`). |
| hub-centric | định tâm bằng moay-ơ | Wheel location primarily centered by the hub pilot. | Correct fit reduces mounting eccentricity. |
| lug-centric | định tâm bằng bu-lông hoặc đai ốc | Wheel location centered by tapered or shaped fasteners. | Requires correct seat type and tightening. |
| wheel stud | bu-lông cấy bánh xe | Threaded fastener fixed to the hub for wheel mounting. | Used with lug nuts. |
| wheel bolt | bu-lông bánh xe | Removable bolt passing through wheel into threaded hub. | Common on some European vehicles. |
| lug nut | đai ốc bánh xe | Nut securing a wheel to a stud. | Seat shape and torque specification matter. |
| tightening torque | mô-men siết | Specified installation torque for a fastener. | `N·m`; sequence and lubrication condition matter. |

## Tire Construction And Markings

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tire | lốp xe | Flexible, pressurized road-contact component mounted on a wheel. | British spelling: `tyre`. |
| tread | mặt gai lốp | Outer rubber region contacting the road. | Pattern and compound affect grip, water evacuation, wear, and noise. |
| tread block | khối gai lốp | Individual raised tread element separated by grooves or sipes. | Deforms under load. |
| groove | rãnh lốp | Channel in the tread that helps manage water, snow, heat, and deformation. | Main and lateral grooves. |
| sipe | rãnh nhỏ trên gai lốp | Thin slit in a tread block that creates edges and changes block flexibility. | Common in wet and winter tires. |
| sidewall | thành lốp | Flexible tire region between tread and bead. | Carries markings and protects carcass. |
| bead | tanh lốp | Reinforced tire edge that seats and seals against the rim. | Contains high-strength bead wire. |
| carcass | lớp bố chịu lực; khung lốp | Main load-carrying cord structure of a tire. | Also called casing. |
| belt | đai lốp | Reinforcement layers beneath the tread controlling shape and stiffness. | Steel belts are common in radial passenger tires. |
| inner liner | lớp lót trong | Low-permeability inner rubber layer retaining air in a tubeless tire. | Replaces a separate inner tube. |
| radial tire | lốp radial; lốp bố tỏa tròn | Tire with carcass cords running approximately radially from bead to bead. | `R` in common size marking. |
| bias-ply tire | lốp bố chéo | Tire with carcass cords crossing diagonally in alternating layers. | Different stiffness and heat behaviour from radial. |
| tubeless tire | lốp không săm | Tire designed to retain air without a separate inner tube. | Requires compatible tire and rim sealing. |
| tire size | kích thước lốp | Standardized code describing nominal section width, aspect ratio, construction, and rim diameter. | Example: `225/45 R17`. |
| section width | chiều rộng danh nghĩa lốp | Nominal maximum tire width under specified rim and measurement conditions. | First number in many metric size codes; `mm`. |
| aspect ratio | tỷ lệ chiều cao thành lốp | Nominal sidewall section height as a percentage of section width. | Second number in `225/45 R17`; percent. |
| load index | chỉ số tải trọng | Code corresponding to maximum permitted tire load under specified conditions. | Must be decoded using a standard table. |
| speed rating | ký hiệu cấp tốc độ | Code indicating maximum speed capability under specified load and test conditions. | Letter code; not a recommended driving speed. |
| treadwear indicator | vạch báo mòn lốp | Raised feature showing when tread reaches a specified minimum groove depth. | Legal minimum varies by jurisdiction. |
| tire compound | hợp chất cao su lốp | Formulated mix of polymers, fillers, oils, curatives, and additives. | Controls grip, wear, temperature, and rolling resistance trade-offs. |

## Tire Behaviour

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| inflation pressure | áp suất bơm lốp | Internal tire pressure relative to a stated reference. | Usually gauge pressure; `kPa`, bar, or `psi`. Measure under specified temperature. |
| cold tire pressure | áp suất lốp nguội | Inflation pressure measured before driving or significant tire heating. | Vehicle placard normally specifies a cold value. |
| contact patch | vùng tiếp xúc lốp–đường | Tire area transmitting normal, longitudinal, and lateral forces to the road. | Pressure distribution is not uniform. |
| normal load | tải trọng thẳng đứng | Force pressing the tire against the road. | `F_z`; newton (`N`). |
| longitudinal force | lực dọc của lốp | Tire force along the wheel's forward direction. | `F_x`; propulsion or braking. |
| lateral force | lực ngang của lốp | Tire force perpendicular to wheel heading in the road plane. | `F_y`; cornering force. |
| slip angle | góc trượt bên | Angle between wheel heading and contact-patch velocity direction. | `α`; degrees or radians. |
| slip ratio | tỷ lệ trượt dọc | Normalized difference between wheel circumferential speed and road-relative speed. | `κ`; definitions and signs vary. |
| self-aligning moment | mô-men tự lựa | Tire moment about the vertical axis generated by distributed contact forces. | `M_z`; `N·m`. |
| pneumatic trail | độ kéo sau khí động của lốp | Effective distance through which lateral force creates self-aligning moment. | Metre (`m`); changes with slip and load. |
| cornering stiffness | độ cứng góc trượt | Initial slope of lateral force versus slip angle. | `C_α`; `N/rad`. |
| longitudinal stiffness | độ cứng trượt dọc | Initial slope of longitudinal force versus slip ratio. | `N` under some normalized definitions; state formulation. |
| load sensitivity | độ nhạy theo tải của lốp | Nonlinear change in tire force capability as normal load changes. | Friction coefficient typically falls as load rises for a given tire. |
| rolling resistance | cản lăn | Energy loss opposing tire rolling, mainly from cyclic deformation. | Force `N` or coefficient `C_rr`. |
| rolling-resistance coefficient | hệ số cản lăn | Rolling-resistance force divided by normal load under a chosen model. | `C_rr`; dimensionless. |
| aquaplaning | hiện tượng trượt nước | Loss of effective tire-road contact when a water layer supports much of the tire load. | Also called hydroplaning. |
| tire temperature | nhiệt độ lốp | Thermal state of tread, carcass, or contained air at a specified location. | `°C`; one surface reading does not describe the whole tire. |
| tire wear | độ mòn lốp | Loss or change of tread material and shape during use. | Can be even, shoulder, center, cupping, or irregular wear. |
| camber thrust | lực ngang do camber | Lateral tire force generated by camber angle even at zero slip angle. | Depends on tire, load, pressure, and camber. |
| tire-pressure monitoring system (TPMS) | hệ thống giám sát áp suất lốp | System warning of tire underinflation or reporting pressure. | `TPMS`; direct systems use pressure sensors, indirect systems infer from wheel behaviour. |
| tire force and moment | lực và mô-men lốp | Full set of forces and moments a tire transmits at its reference frame. | Common components: `F_x`, `F_y`, `F_z`, `M_x`, `M_y`, `M_z`. |
