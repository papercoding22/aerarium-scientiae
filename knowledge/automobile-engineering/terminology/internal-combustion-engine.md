## Core Principles

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| internal-combustion engine (ICE) | động cơ đốt trong | Heat engine in which combustion occurs inside the working chamber. | `ICE`. Petrol and diesel piston engines are common types. |
| reciprocating engine | động cơ piston tịnh tiến | Engine that uses pistons moving back and forth in cylinders. | Piston motion is converted into crankshaft rotation. |
| spark-ignition engine (SI) | động cơ đánh lửa cưỡng bức | Engine in which a spark initiates combustion of the air-fuel mixture. | `SI`; commonly petrol but other fuels are possible. |
| compression-ignition engine (CI) | động cơ cháy do nén; động cơ diesel | Engine in which fuel ignites because compressed air is hot. | `CI`; normally associated with diesel operation. |
| four-stroke cycle | chu trình bốn kỳ | Operating cycle completed in intake, compression, power, and exhaust strokes. | Two crankshaft revolutions per cycle. |
| two-stroke cycle | chu trình hai kỳ | Operating cycle completed in two piston strokes. | One crankshaft revolution per cycle. |
| intake stroke | kỳ nạp | Piston movement that draws fresh charge into the cylinder. | First stroke of a common four-stroke cycle. |
| compression stroke | kỳ nén | Piston movement that compresses the cylinder charge. | Occurs before ignition or fuel combustion. |
| power stroke | kỳ cháy giãn nở; kỳ sinh công | Expansion stroke during which cylinder gas does positive work on the piston. | Also called expansion stroke. |
| exhaust stroke | kỳ xả | Piston movement that pushes exhaust gas out of the cylinder. | Fourth stroke of a common four-stroke cycle. |
| Otto cycle | chu trình Otto | Idealized thermodynamic cycle often used to model spark-ignition engines. | Ideal heat addition is at constant volume. |
| Diesel cycle | chu trình Diesel | Idealized thermodynamic cycle often used to model compression-ignition engines. | Ideal heat addition is at constant pressure. |
| top dead center (TDC) | điểm chết trên | Highest piston position in its cylinder. | `TDC`; crank angle reference. |
| bottom dead center (BDC) | điểm chết dưới | Lowest piston position in its cylinder. | `BDC`. |
| bore | đường kính xy lanh | Internal cylinder diameter. | `B`; millimetre (`mm`). |
| stroke | hành trình piston | Piston travel from TDC to BDC. | `S`; millimetre (`mm`). |
| swept volume | thể tích công tác | Volume displaced by a piston moving through one stroke. | Per cylinder or total; `cm³` or `L`. |
| engine displacement | dung tích công tác động cơ | Sum of swept volumes of all cylinders. | Often called engine capacity; `cm³` or litre (`L`). |
| clearance volume | thể tích buồng cháy còn lại | Cylinder volume remaining above the piston at TDC. | Used to calculate compression ratio. |
| compression ratio | tỷ số nén | Ratio of maximum cylinder volume to minimum cylinder volume. | `r = (V_s + V_c)/V_c`; dimensionless. |
| firing order | thứ tự nổ | Sequence in which cylinders produce their power events. | Chosen for smoothness, loads, and packaging. |
| engine cycle | chu trình động cơ | Repeating sequence of gas-exchange, compression, combustion, and expansion processes. | Do not confuse with one crankshaft revolution. |

## Main Mechanical Parts

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cylinder block | thân máy; khối xy lanh | Main engine structure containing cylinders, oil and coolant passages, and crankcase features. | Often cast iron or aluminium alloy. |
| cylinder head | nắp máy; nắp quy lát | Structure closing the cylinders and often containing valves, ports, camshafts, and combustion chambers. | Sealed to the block by a head gasket. |
| cylinder liner | ống lót xy lanh | Wear-resistant surface forming the cylinder bore. | Wet or dry liner; not every engine has a separate liner. |
| combustion chamber | buồng cháy | Space in which the air-fuel charge burns. | Shape affects combustion, knock, emissions, and efficiency. |
| piston | piston; pít-tông | Sliding component that receives gas pressure and transfers force to the connecting rod. | Usually aluminium alloy in passenger-car engines. |
| piston ring | xéc-măng; vòng găng piston | Ring sealing combustion gas, controlling oil, and transferring heat from piston to cylinder. | Compression rings and oil-control ring. |
| gudgeon pin | chốt piston | Pin connecting the piston to the connecting rod small end. | Also called wrist pin or piston pin. |
| connecting rod | thanh truyền; tay biên | Link transferring force between piston and crankshaft. | Has small end and big end. |
| crankshaft | trục khuỷu | Shaft converting reciprocating piston force into rotary torque. | Supported by main bearings. |
| crankpin | chốt khuỷu | Offset crankshaft journal to which a connecting rod big end attaches. | Offset determines half the stroke. |
| main bearing | ổ trục chính; bạc trục chính | Bearing supporting the crankshaft in the engine block. | Hydrodynamic plain bearings are common. |
| crankcase | cácte trục khuỷu; hộp trục khuỷu | Engine structure surrounding the crankshaft. | Sometimes `crankcase` includes the lower block region. |
| flywheel | bánh đà | Rotating mass that smooths speed variation and provides a clutch or converter interface. | Stores rotational kinetic energy. |
| torsional vibration damper | bộ giảm chấn xoắn | Device reducing crankshaft torsional vibration. | Often mounted at the crankshaft front; also called harmonic balancer. |
| valvetrain | cơ cấu phối khí | Mechanism that operates intake and exhaust valves with controlled timing and lift. | May include camshaft, followers, rockers, valves, and springs. |
| camshaft | trục cam | Shaft with lobes that controls valve events. | Driven at half crankshaft speed in a four-stroke engine. |
| cam lobe | vấu cam | Shaped camshaft feature that produces valve lift. | Profile controls lift, velocity, and duration. |
| tappet | con đội | Component following a cam profile and transmitting motion in a valvetrain. | Also called lifter or cam follower, depending on design. |
| rocker arm | cò mổ; đòn gánh xu-páp | Pivoted lever transmitting cam motion to a valve. | Not used in every valvetrain layout. |
| intake valve | xu-páp nạp | Valve controlling flow from the intake port into a cylinder. | Valve timing is measured in crank angle degrees. |
| exhaust valve | xu-páp xả | Valve controlling flow from a cylinder into the exhaust port. | Operates at high thermal load. |
| valve spring | lò xo xu-páp | Spring that closes a valve and keeps valvetrain parts in contact. | Must avoid valve float at intended speed. |
| valve lift | độ nâng xu-páp | Maximum or instantaneous distance a valve moves from its seat. | Millimetre (`mm`). |
| valve timing | thời điểm phối khí | Crankshaft angles at which valves open and close. | Expressed in crank-angle degrees. |
| valve overlap | trùng điệp xu-páp | Period near exhaust TDC when intake and exhaust valves are both open. | Crank-angle degrees. |
| variable valve timing (VVT) | điều khiển thời điểm phối khí biến thiên | System that changes valve timing during operation. | `VVT`; may vary cam phase, duration, or both. |
| variable valve lift (VVL) | điều khiển độ nâng xu-páp biến thiên | System that changes valve lift during operation. | `VVL`. |
| timing belt | dây đai cam | Toothed belt synchronizing crankshaft and camshaft rotation. | Has a specified replacement or inspection strategy. |
| timing chain | xích cam | Chain synchronizing crankshaft and camshaft rotation. | Requires guides, tensioner, and lubrication. |
| head gasket | gioăng quy lát; gioăng nắp máy | Seal between cylinder block and head for combustion gas, coolant, and oil passages. | Failure can mix fluids or lose compression. |

## Air Fuel And Combustion

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| intake manifold | ống góp nạp; cổ góp nạp | Passage system distributing intake air or mixture to cylinders. | Runner geometry affects airflow and tuning. |
| exhaust manifold | ống góp xả; cổ góp xả | Passage system collecting exhaust gas from cylinders. | Pulse interaction and temperature are important. |
| throttle | bướm ga | Valve controlling intake airflow in many spark-ignition engines. | Usually inside a throttle body. |
| throttle body | thân bướm ga | Housing containing the throttle valve and often its actuator and sensors. | Electronic throttle is also called drive-by-wire throttle. |
| air-fuel ratio (AFR) | tỷ lệ không khí–nhiên liệu | Mass ratio of air to fuel in the cylinder charge or mixture. | `AFR`; dimensionless mass ratio. |
| stoichiometric mixture | hỗn hợp đúng tỷ lượng | Mixture containing the chemically required air for complete ideal fuel combustion. | Petrol is often approximated near `14.7:1` by mass; fuel composition changes the value. |
| lambda | hệ số dư lượng không khí; lambda | Actual AFR divided by stoichiometric AFR under a common convention. | `λ`; dimensionless. `λ = 1` is stoichiometric. |
| rich mixture | hỗn hợp giàu | Mixture with more fuel relative to air than stoichiometric. | Commonly `λ < 1` for the stated convention. |
| lean mixture | hỗn hợp nghèo | Mixture with less fuel relative to air than stoichiometric. | Commonly `λ > 1`. |
| fuel injector | kim phun nhiên liệu | Metering valve that sprays fuel when electronically or mechanically commanded. | Characterized by flow rate and spray pattern. |
| port fuel injection (PFI) | phun nhiên liệu đường ống nạp | Injection into the intake port upstream of the intake valve. | `PFI`; also called multipoint injection in multi-injector systems. |
| gasoline direct injection (GDI) | phun xăng trực tiếp | High-pressure petrol injection directly into the combustion chamber. | `GDI`. |
| common-rail injection | phun nhiên liệu đường ống chung | High-pressure injection system using a shared accumulator rail. | Common in modern diesel engines. |
| fuel rail | ống phân phối nhiên liệu | Manifold supplying pressurized fuel to injectors. | Pressure depends on injection system. |
| spark plug | bugi đánh lửa | Device producing an electrical spark in a spark-ignition engine. | Spark gap and heat range are important specifications. |
| ignition timing | thời điểm đánh lửa | Crank angle at which the spark is commanded relative to piston position. | Degrees before or after TDC. |
| fuel injection timing | thời điểm phun nhiên liệu | Crank angle or time at which injection begins, ends, or occurs. | Definition must state start, end, or center of injection. |
| combustion | sự cháy; quá trình cháy | Chemical reaction releasing heat from fuel and oxidizer. | Rate and phasing affect torque, efficiency, and emissions. |
| flame front | màng lửa; mặt lửa | Boundary region through which an unburned mixture becomes burned gas. | Important in spark-ignition combustion. |
| knock | kích nổ | Abnormal combustion involving autoignition of unburned end gas and pressure oscillation. | Often detected by vibration or cylinder-pressure analysis. |
| detonation | nổ kích; kích nổ | Very rapid combustion with strong pressure waves. In automotive usage, often used loosely for severe knock. | Usage can vary; prefer the exact combustion definition. |
| pre-ignition | cháy sớm | Ignition of the charge before the commanded spark, caused by a hot source. | Different from knock, though one can lead to the other. |
| octane rating | trị số octan | Measure of a petrol fuel's resistance to knock under defined tests. | `RON`, `MON`, or anti-knock index must be stated. |
| cetane number | trị số xetan | Measure of diesel fuel ignition quality under defined conditions. | Higher value generally means shorter ignition delay. |
| exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) | tuần hoàn khí xả | Controlled return of exhaust gas to reduce combustion temperature and emissions. | `EGR`; internal or external. |

## Boosting And Exhaust

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| naturally aspirated engine | động cơ nạp khí tự nhiên | Engine that fills cylinders without a compressor raising intake pressure above ambient. | `NA` is a common informal abbreviation. |
| forced induction | nạp cưỡng bức | Use of a compressor to increase intake-air density and engine charge. | Includes turbocharging and supercharging. |
| boost pressure | áp suất tăng áp | Intake pressure rise produced by a compressor, often stated as gauge pressure. | `kPa` or bar; confirm gauge versus absolute. |
| turbocharger | bộ tăng áp khí xả | Compressor driven by an exhaust-gas turbine on a connected shaft. | Common abbreviation `turbo`. |
| turbine | tua-bin | Turbocharger wheel extracting energy from exhaust gas. | Drives the compressor through a shaft. |
| compressor | máy nén; bánh nén | Turbocharger or supercharger stage that raises intake-air pressure. | Compressor maps use pressure ratio and corrected flow. |
| turbo lag | độ trễ tăng áp | Delay between increased driver demand and useful boost response. | A transient response, not one fixed time in all conditions. |
| wastegate | van điều khiển khí xả qua tua-bin | Valve bypassing exhaust around a turbo turbine to control speed and boost. | Internal or external. |
| variable-geometry turbocharger (VGT) | bộ tăng áp hình học biến thiên | Turbocharger with adjustable turbine flow geometry. | `VGT` or `VNT`; improves operating-range control. |
| supercharger | bộ siêu nạp dẫn động cơ khí | Intake compressor driven mechanically by the engine or another power source. | Drive power is taken from the engine in common designs. |
| intercooler | bộ làm mát khí nạp | Heat exchanger cooling compressed intake air before it enters the engine. | Air-to-air or liquid-cooled. |
| exhaust aftertreatment | hệ thống xử lý khí thải sau động cơ | Devices that chemically or physically reduce pollutants after combustion. | Includes catalysts and particulate filters. |
| catalytic converter | bộ chuyển đổi xúc tác; bộ xúc tác khí thải | Reactor using catalyst materials to convert harmful exhaust constituents. | Petrol and diesel systems use different combinations. |
| three-way catalyst (TWC) | bộ xúc tác ba chức năng | Catalyst reducing `NOx` while oxidizing `CO` and hydrocarbons near stoichiometric operation. | `TWC`. |
| diesel oxidation catalyst (DOC) | bộ xúc tác oxy hóa diesel | Catalyst oxidizing carbon monoxide, hydrocarbons, and some exhaust species. | `DOC`. |
| diesel particulate filter (DPF) | bộ lọc hạt diesel | Filter trapping diesel particulate matter and periodically oxidizing it. | `DPF`; cleaning process is regeneration. |
| gasoline particulate filter (GPF) | bộ lọc hạt xăng | Filter reducing particulate emissions from petrol engines. | `GPF`. |
| selective catalytic reduction (SCR) | khử xúc tác chọn lọc | Aftertreatment that reduces `NOx` using a reducing agent, commonly ammonia formed from urea solution. | `SCR`. |
| regeneration | tái sinh bộ lọc | Process of removing accumulated particulate matter from a filter by oxidation. | Passive, active, or service regeneration. |
| back pressure | áp suất ngược | Pressure opposing exhaust or intake flow relative to a reference. | Excessive exhaust back pressure can reduce performance. |

## Lubrication And Cooling

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lubricant | chất bôi trơn | Substance reducing friction, wear, and heat between moving surfaces. | Engine oil also cleans, seals, and protects. |
| engine oil | dầu động cơ; dầu máy | Formulated lubricant used in an engine. | Viscosity grade and performance specification must match requirements. |
| oil viscosity grade | cấp độ nhớt dầu | Classification describing oil viscosity behaviour under defined tests. | Example: `SAE 0W-20`; not a direct viscosity value. |
| oil pump | bơm dầu bôi trơn | Pump circulating oil through the lubrication system. | Common types include gear and gerotor pumps. |
| oil gallery | đường dầu | Internal passage carrying pressurized oil to engine components. | Located in block, head, crankshaft, or other parts. |
| oil filter | lọc dầu | Filter removing contaminants from circulating oil. | Bypass valve can maintain flow if restriction is high. |
| oil sump | cácte dầu; đáy dầu | Reservoir collecting engine oil beneath or beside the crankcase. | Wet-sump or dry-sump architecture. |
| wet-sump system | hệ thống cácte ướt | Lubrication system storing most oil in the engine sump. | Common in passenger cars. |
| dry-sump system | hệ thống cácte khô | System scavenging oil into a separate tank. | Supports oil control under high acceleration and flexible packaging. |
| coolant | chất làm mát; nước làm mát | Heat-transfer fluid circulating through the engine and cooling system. | Usually water plus antifreeze and additives. |
| cooling jacket | áo nước làm mát | Coolant passages around hot engine regions. | Commonly around cylinders and combustion chambers. |
| radiator | két nước; bộ tản nhiệt | Heat exchanger transferring coolant heat to ambient air. | Radiator cap and airflow system affect operation. |
| thermostat | van hằng nhiệt | Temperature-controlled valve regulating coolant flow paths. | Helps the engine warm up and controls temperature. |
| water pump | bơm nước làm mát | Pump circulating engine coolant. | Mechanically or electrically driven. |
| cooling fan | quạt làm mát | Fan moving air through heat exchangers when vehicle airflow is insufficient. | Often electrically controlled. |
| thermal management | quản lý nhiệt | Coordinated control of heat generation, transfer, storage, and rejection. | Applies to engine, cabin, battery, electronics, and driveline. |

## Engine Performance

| English term | Common Vietnamese | Engineering meaning | Symbol, unit, or note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| indicated power | công suất chỉ thị | Power developed by gas pressure inside the cylinders. | `P_i`; derived from cylinder pressure. |
| brake power | công suất có ích tại trục | Usable power measured at the engine output shaft. | `P_b`; `kW`. `Brake` refers to a dynamometer. |
| friction power | công suất tổn hao cơ khí | Difference between indicated power and brake power. | Includes friction and pumping/accessory losses under the chosen method. |
| indicated mean effective pressure (IMEP) | áp suất có ích trung bình chỉ thị | Fictitious constant pressure producing the indicated work over displacement. | `IMEP`; `Pa` or bar. |
| brake mean effective pressure (BMEP) | áp suất có ích trung bình tại trục | Fictitious constant pressure relating measured brake torque to displacement. | `BMEP`; `Pa` or bar. Useful for comparing engine loading. |
| volumetric efficiency | hiệu suất nạp | Ratio comparing actual inducted air mass with a reference amount based on displacement and ambient state. | `η_v`; definition varies, dimensionless or percent. |
| thermal efficiency | hiệu suất nhiệt | Ratio of useful work output to fuel chemical energy input. | `η_th`; dimensionless or percent. |
| mechanical efficiency | hiệu suất cơ khí | Ratio of brake power to indicated power under a stated condition. | `η_m`; dimensionless or percent. |
| brake-specific fuel consumption (BSFC) | suất tiêu hao nhiên liệu có ích | Fuel mass flow divided by brake power. Lower values mean less fuel for the same power. | `BSFC`; commonly `g/kWh`. |
| engine speed | tốc độ động cơ; số vòng tua | Crankshaft rotational speed. | Revolutions per minute (`rpm`). |
| idle speed | tốc độ không tải | Engine speed while running without driver-requested propulsion load. | `rpm`; accessories and warm-up can change it. |
| redline | giới hạn vòng tua | Marked or controlled upper engine-speed region. | `rpm`; not necessarily the mechanical failure speed. |
| torque curve | đường đặc tính mô-men xoắn | Graph of engine output torque versus speed under stated conditions. | `N·m` versus `rpm`. |
| power curve | đường đặc tính công suất | Graph of engine output power versus speed under stated conditions. | `kW` versus `rpm`. |
| engine map | bản đồ đặc tính động cơ | Multidimensional data describing engine output, efficiency, emissions, or control values. | Axes often include speed and load. |
| engine load | tải động cơ | Measure of demanded or produced engine output relative to a reference. | Definition varies: torque, air charge, pressure, or normalized value. |
| dynamometer | băng thử công suất; lực kế động lực | Machine that applies and measures torque, speed, and power. | Engine dyno or chassis dyno. |
