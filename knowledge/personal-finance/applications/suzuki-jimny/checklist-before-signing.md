## Before You Sign

- [ ] Loan amount, rate, and term in the contract match the letter (487M, 9.75%, 96 months).
- [ ] Early repayment fee is written in the contract.
- [ ] Monthly budget still works if the rate rises to 12% (≈ 7.85M/month from month 13, estimate).
- [ ] Final car price in the sales contract is known; down payment recalculated if it changed.
- [ ] Keep a copy or photo of every document you sign.

## After Disbursement

- [ ] Confirm TFSVN paid the dealer.
- [ ] Register the car in my name.
- [ ] Send the original registration (and vehicle file, if any) to TFSVN right after receiving it.
- [ ] Update [overview.md](overview.md) with contract number, disbursement date, and first payment date.
