# Car Loan Rate Changes

## What To Do At Each Reset

1. Get the new rate and new monthly payment from TFSVN (notice, app, or call).
2. Add a row to [Rate History](#rate-history).
3. Update the current rate and payment in [overview.md](overview.md).
4. Check the monthly budget still works.
5. If the rate went up a lot, compare with paying early (see the early repayment fee in [overview.md](overview.md)).
