# Car Loan Payment Log

One row per monthly payment. Use it to see how much you have paid, how much was interest, and what you still owe.

## How To Fill A Row

Copy the numbers from the TFSVN statement or payment notice when you have them. If you don't, you can estimate:

1. **Interest** = balance before payment × yearly rate ÷ 12
2. **Principal** = amount paid − interest
3. **Balance after** = balance before − principal

Example (month 1, estimate): 487,000,000 × 9.75% ÷ 12 ≈ 3,956,875 interest. If you pay 7,325,553, principal ≈ 3,368,678, and the balance after ≈ 483,631,322.

Mark estimated rows with `(est.)` and replace them with the real numbers from TFSVN when you get them.
